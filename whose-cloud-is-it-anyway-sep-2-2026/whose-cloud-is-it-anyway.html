BYOD

Bring Your Own Data

Not Device. Nobody has lost sleep over Device since 2014.

D is Data. Specifically, the loudest data you own: telemetry.

Logs, metrics, traces. The only dataset you generate faster than you can decide where it's allowed to live.

// no dog was harmed in the making of this acronym. several petabytes were.

TDP

Telemetry Data Platform

One set of decisions about the telemetry, made once. One schema, one policy surface, one bill.

Your UI, your alerts, your agents are clients of that data. Not owners of four copies of it.

// and yes, it still runs in your cloud. that part was never negotiable. that's the C.
